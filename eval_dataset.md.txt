Case 1 环境科学：土壤重金属修复（完整标准摘要）
Soil heavy‑metal pollution threatens crop safety in agricultural regions. This study explores the remediation effect of biochar derived from rice husk on cadmium‑contaminated farmland soil. A series of pot planting experiments are carried out with different biochar application ratios. Experimental results indicate that appropriate biochar addition can reduce cadmium uptake by lettuce shoots. One main restriction is that the remediation performance varies greatly under different soil pH conditions.
Case 2 食品科学：果蔬保鲜（完整标准摘要）
Postharvest decay limits the shelf‑life of fresh blueberries. This research investigates the preservation effect of chitosan edible coating combined with tea polyphenols. Physiological indicators including weight loss rate and fruit firmness are measured during storage. The composite coating effectively inhibits mold growth and extends storage time. The disadvantage is that high concentration coating will cause slight surface discoloration of fruit.
